# AI Executive Team

Internal AI workforce for Vexel Systems.

## Executive Workers

### Strategic Advisor
Evaluates opportunities, priorities, risks, and founder focus.

### Research Analyst
Identifies profitable industries, operational bottlenecks, and portfolio opportunities.

### Product Manager
Transforms business problems into MVPs, scopes, repository structures, and delivery plans.

### Lead Qualification Specialist
Protects sales capacity by qualifying and routing prospects.

### Documentation Manager
Maintains repositories, SOPs, workflow documentation, and internal knowledge.

## Core Principle

Every worker exists to help Vexel Systems:

1. Land clients
2. Build portfolio assets
3. Deliver projects
4. Generate revenue

No worker exists for vanity projects, hype, or unnecessary complexity.
